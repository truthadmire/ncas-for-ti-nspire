# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Full-screen mode: the red cursor on the entry line was drawn about one
  character to the right of where typing goes (since the 2D entry line in
  1.0), so `eigVc([|])` looked like `eigVc([]|)`, and reaching the inside of
  brackets meant putting the cursor after the closing one. The cursor is now
  drawn exactly where the next character goes, in plain text and inside
  templates. New test `tests/cursor_check.py` compares the cursor with where a
  typed character actually lands, after every key.
- navcalc and unitconv: a value is no longer limited to digits, `.`, `E` and
  the sign. Fields take sums with `+ − × ÷ ^ ( )` and π (`120+15`, `3/4`,
  `2^10`, `2(1+π)`); an unfinished end such as `12+` is left out until it is
  finished; **(−)** still changes the sign of the whole value. Keys are also
  recognised under other names a system may report (`decimal`, `point`,
  `minus`, `times`, `divide`, ...), in ncasgui too. navcalc is now about 21 KB
  (two 20,000-character pastes); unitconv about 15 KB (one).
- Website: the live demos were redrawn with the corrected cursor; monospace
  text no longer turns `<=` and `<-` into ligature arrows.
- Website: new **usage guide** page (`site/guide.html`): all five programs,
  key by key, with 36 real screens (each made by sending the keys shown to
  the program on the simulated calculator), text-mode transcripts, key and
  ctrl tables, typing rules, troubleshooting and every command's syntax.
  Generated by `tools/guide.py`; CI checks it is up to date.
- Full-screen mode, faster editing:
  - A typed `)` `]` `}` steps over the same bracket when it is already there
    (function keys type both brackets), and `)` at the end of √, |x|, an
    n-th root or d/dx leaves the template, so no extra brackets pile up.
  - Brackets left open are closed at enter, as on the CAS: `factor(x^2-1`
    works. Also in ncastext and ncasx; the history shows the closed input.
  - **ctrl ◄ / ►** jump to the start / end of the line; **shift ◄ / ►**
    move over a whole name, number or template.
  - **var** lists `ans` and your variables and functions with their values;
    enter types the name.
  - The ctrl help page (ctrl trig) lists the new keys and fits the screen.
  - Nothing is stored for these: a forced exit loses nothing.
- Catalog and help: command names use TI's capitalisation everywhere
  (`cSolve`, `comDenom`, `sumSeq`, ...), also in "did you mean" hints.

## 1.2

- New separate program **unitconv**, a unit converter with a full-screen
  form: 16 categories (length, area, volume, mass, temperature, time, speed,
  pressure, energy, power, force, angle, data size, fuel economy, density,
  frequency), about 150 units with exact factors. Type a value in any unit and
  every other unit converts as you type. `onefile/unitconv.py` (~13 KB),
  `src/unitconv.py`, tests in `tests/unit_check.py`.
- New separate program **navcalc**, an aviation (E6B) calculator with
  full-screen forms: wind triangle (any two blanks, also finds the wind),
  crosswind, time-speed-distance, fuel, pressure/density altitude, CAS/TAS/Mach,
  true altitude, cloud base and unit conversions. Blank fields are solved as
  you type (closed forms where possible, otherwise Newton's method); left/right
  change a field's unit. `onefile/navcalc.py` (~19 KB), `src/navcalc.py`,
  tests in `tests/nav_check.py`.
- Full-screen mode: calculus templates in menu ▸ 9 Templates: **a** ∫ f dx,
  **b** ∫ with lower and upper limits (▲ / ▼ move between them), **c** d/dx.
  Integrals and derivatives in the history (typed or as results) are drawn
  with ∫, limits and d/dx.
- Website (`site/`) with live demos of the templates.

Memory:
- Size budgets checked before the work: expansions over 2000 terms, matrices
  over 2500 entries, lists over 5000 elements and polynomials of degree 2000+
  stop at once with "Resource limit: ..." instead of exhausting memory.
- Full-screen mode: history limited by size (30 entries, about 6000
  characters) as well as count; undo limited to 30 steps and 3000 characters,
  with a run of typing as one step; layouts of dropped entries are evicted;
  only the visible history is laid out.
- Running out of memory while calculating, drawing or editing is recovered:
  history shortened, undo and layouts cleared, variables kept, nCAS keeps
  running. New commands `:clearans`, `:clearhist`, `:free`.
- Fractions are cross-cancelled before multiplying (and added over the least
  common denominator), keeping intermediate integers small; matrix routines
  no longer copy their rows twice.
- The single-file programs resume a lazily loaded part (help, menus,
  full-screen mode) where it stopped if loading ran out of memory.
- `approx()` and Approx mode compute numeric expressions with large exact
  parts in decimals directly, falling back to exact arithmetic when digits
  would cancel.
- New tests: `tests/mem_soak.py` (long session on MicroPython's heap) and
  `tests/oom_check.py` (recovery), both in CI.

## 1.1

Wrong answers fixed:
- `solve` with inequalities joined by `and`, chained inequalities (`1<2x+1<7`)
  and lists of inequalities no longer return `false`; `|` conditions now apply
  to inequalities too (`solve(x^2<9,x)|x>0` gives `0<x<3`). Unions are merged
  (`solve(x>1 or x<3,x)` gives `true`).
- `piecewise(e1,c1,e2,c2)` (the template form) no longer gives `undef`;
  piecewise functions integrate, take limits and solve exactly.
- Divergent integrals give `undef`, `∞` or `-∞` instead of a number
  (`integral(1/x,x,-1,1)`, `integral(tan(x),x,0,pi)`); poles of tan, sec and
  transcendental denominators are found. Numeric integrals that do not
  converge report "Questionable accuracy" instead of a wrong value;
  oscillatory integrals such as `integral(sin(x)/x,x,0,inf)` are extrapolated.
- Substitutions no longer lose the sign for x<0 (`integral(x^2/sqrt(9-x^2),x,-3,3)`
  gave 0, now 9π/2); trigonometric substitution for sqrt(a²−x²), sqrt(x²+a²),
  sqrt(x²−a²); definite integrals split at |…| sign changes.
- Limits: factorials (`limit(n!/2^n,n,inf)`), floor/ceiling one-sided limits,
  squeeze cases (`limit(x*sin(1/x),x,0)`), exponential vs power, and
  `limit(1/ln(x),x,inf)` (was ∞, now 0).
- Infinite series: divergence test, alternating series by extrapolation and
  Euler–Maclaurin tail for positive terms (`sumSeq(1/k^1.5,k,1,inf)` is now
  correct to 10 digits; `sumSeq(1/sqrt(k),k,1,inf)` gives ∞).
- Exact constants that agree to 12 digits compare equal (`ln(8)/ln(2)=3` is
  `true`); identities such as `(x+1)^2=x^2+2x+1` give `true`.
- `solve(sin(x)*cos(x)=1/4,x)` and similar trig products are solved exactly.
- Absolute-value equations are solved by sign cases: exact roots and whole
  intervals (`solve(abs(x)+abs(x-2)=2,x)` gives `0≤x≤2`).
- `fMax`/`fMin`/`nfMax`/`nfMin` of unbounded functions give ±∞ instead of
  every critical point.
- `eigVl` lists repeated eigenvalues and accepts letters; decimal matrices
  with repeated eigenvalues give clean results.

New:
- Integrals of sec^n and csc^n, sec·tan, csc·cot, rational functions of sin
  and cos (tan(x/2) substitution, with the jump at x=π handled in definite
  integrals), sinh² and cosh², 1/(x⁴+1) and other quartics without real roots.
- Inequalities with a letter coefficient: `solve(a*x<5,x)` gives one answer
  for each sign of `a`.
- Settings, variables and functions are kept in the document (list variable
  `ncasmem`) and come back after Ctrl+R or reopening the document.
- Full-screen mode: the command syntax shows in the status bar while typing
  its arguments and under the catalog list; results taller than the screen
  scroll line by line.
- Wrong-number-of-arguments errors name the command and show its syntax;
  near-miss names get a hint (`factr` → did you mean factor?); `diff`,
  `deriv`, `integrate`, `avg` and `cbrt` are accepted.
- Text mode prompt shows the active modes (`deg approx> `).

Fixes:
- The module install no longer crashes when `casgui.py` is missing (it falls
  back to text mode); the launcher checks `casgui` too.
- `gui()` and `menu()` typed in the Shell work in the single-file builds.
- Lower memory use: the numeric root scan no longer builds large lists, so
  the self-test passes with a 0.7 MB (ncastext) or 0.9 MB (ncasgui) heap.
- Huge exact roots (`1000000^(101/100)`) no longer stall.
- `tools/paste_parts.py` works on Windows and Linux (clip, wl-copy, xclip,
  xsel, or part files) and names the right document.
